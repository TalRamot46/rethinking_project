mean_shift / max_shift : distance the estimate moved
spread_emp             : mean distance from the population mean, raw
spread_est             : mean distance from the population mean, pooled
shrink_factor          : 1 - spread_est/spread_emp

population mean rating implied by a_bar: 4.786 

mean_shift is not monotone in the review count, and that is not a bug.
A one-review listing is nearly always rated exactly 5.0, which already
sits close to the population mean, so it has little distance to move
even though nearly all of that distance is taken away from it.
shrink_factor is the measure that answers the intended question.
